# MOOVKA MASTER ENVIRONMENT v02

Status: LOCKED.

Master image: `MOOVKA_MASTER_ENVIRONMENT_v02.png` (1536 × 1024 px).

## Locked environment

- Empty modern exercise studio.
- Almost-white neutral wall; the wall must not read as gray.
- Light natural light-oak / warm-neutral floor with restrained realistic grain.
- Clean white baseboard.
- Translucent white curtain only at the far left.
- Soft daylight from the left.
- Only a very subtle, smooth light gradient is allowed on the wall.
- Neutral, gently welcoming color balance: no beige/yellow warmth and no cold blue cast.
- Level front-facing architecture with straight verticals and horizontals.
- **NO VISIBLE SHADOW PATTERN ON WALL.**

## Forbidden

- Logo or text on the wall.
- Plants, shelves, books, towels, vases, artwork, mirrors, furniture, outlets, panels, or any decor.
- Visible ceiling, ceiling light strip, recessed cove, architectural light strip, or any ceiling light feature.
- Any light or shadow pattern on the wall, including diagonal bands, blind shadows, window-frame shadows, curtain shadows, or hard light patches.
- Stains, blotches, flecks, dirty plaster, warped corners, distorted floorboards, CGI/waxy texture, vignette, or watermark.

## Camera derivation rule

- This image locks architecture, materials, light, and real-world room proportions; it is not a pixel-identical background for every pose.
- Standing, quadruped, and lying cameras may frame the room differently only according to their locked camera references.
- Use the separate locked standing, quadruped, and lying camera calibration references for model scale and placement.
- The physical 183 × 68 cm mat is the primary scale authority for model size, camera distance, room scale, and cross-exercise consistency. The model is 180 cm tall.
- If pose, model, or camera scale does not fit the mat, correct the model or camera; never shorten, lengthen, or rescale the mat.
